How to play

Circles expand on the screen. Tap at the right moment to stop each circle as close as possible to the target without overlapping the others. Stop all the circles in a level to clear the stage. The more precise your timing, the higher your score.

Frequently asked questions

Does Circle Cycle need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline.

Where are my top scores saved?

Your top scores are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.

I deleted the app. Can I get my scores back?

Because scores are saved locally, deleting the app also deletes them and they can't be restored.

Taps feel delayed or the game stutters.

Close other apps running in the background, turn off Low Power Mode (iOS) or Battery Saver (Android), and make sure you have the latest version of Circle Cycle and your operating system installed. If the issue continues, email us with your device model.

How do I reset the game?

Deleting and reinstalling the app resets all progress and scores.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Circle Cycle mobile app for iOS and Android ("the App") handles information. In short: the App does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and top scores locally on your device so you can see them the next time you play. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Deleting the App removes this data.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Crash reports

Depending on your device settings, the platform provider may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us: Apple on iOS (if you have opted in to share analytics with app developers) and Google on Android (through Google Play's diagnostics). This information does not identify you and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy respectively.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Since we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by deleting the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
